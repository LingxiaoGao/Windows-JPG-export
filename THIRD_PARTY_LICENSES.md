Build dependency versions are pinned in `requirements-build.txt`. PyInstaller dependencies are build-only, not all shipped at runtime. The A98 RGB compatible ICC is generated by our own code from the public color-space specification, not a redistributed Adobe ICC binary. Color-space definitions: https://www.adobe.com/digitalimag/pdfs/AdobeRGB1998.pdf and https://www.w3.org/TR/css-color/#predefined-a98-rgb .

No source/user photos, rawpy, LibRaw, cloud account tokens, or telemetry are part of the portable application. SHA-256 manifests verify the distributed files; they are not a code-signing certificate.
